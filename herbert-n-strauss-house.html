9 East 71st Street, Lenox Hill. Seven-story limestone townhouse, 1933, by Trowbridge & Livingston for Herbert N. Straus of R.H. Macy & Co. Neo-French Renaissance — the idiom of Vanderbilt, Frick, Duke, already retrospective by 1933.

Standard Beaux-Arts plan: controlled threshold, piano nobile above, private quarters on upper floors, service circulation in a parallel stack. Checkerboard marble entry hall, oak-paneled reception rooms, Persian carpets, leather-bound library.
